Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers to common questions about our services, response times, support options, and payment arrangements.

Is this company registered?

Yes. We are registered as a corporation under the name Tech Bridge Liberia (TBL) Group of Companies.

What services do you offer?

We provide website development, web app and PWA development, data collection and analysis services, Microsoft Office training, IT consultancy, CCTV camera installation, digital literacy training, software and hardware troubleshooting, and software installation.

How quickly can you respond?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days. For urgent technical matters, we may respond within 2 to 4 hours depending on the request and availability.

Do you offer remote support?

Yes. We provide remote support for eligible software issues, technical guidance, and consultations. On-site support is available for hardware work, installations, inspections, and location-based services.

What are your payment terms?

We accept bank transfers, mobile money, and cash payments. Final payment terms depend on the service scope, project duration, and any materials or on-site requirements involved.
